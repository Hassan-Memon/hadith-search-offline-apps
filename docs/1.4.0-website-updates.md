# 1.4.0 — the website's updates of 12–13 November

Date: 13 November 2026. App version 1.4.0, data schema 3 (unchanged).

The website (repository `hadithsearch`, PRs #115, #119 and #120) changed its
navigation, home page, narration and narrator pages, its PDF library and how
it shows numbers and honorifics. This release brings them into the app. No
data schema change and no new download: the data builder only reorders the
PDF list and names its books.

### Not brought over

- **/about's "you are reading" bar and self-scrolling contents** (0d35d6a). The
  app's contents is a folded list at the top; a reading-position bar is a
  separate piece of work.
- **The pager's direction and labels, result ranges from page 2** (db2d6f8).
  The app has no pager: lists grow with «مزید دکھائیں».
- **Security headers, CI tests, server-side book list** (0596195, 01ed2fb).
  Website-server work.

## 2. Checks

- Core tests: 41 pass, 6 of them new (`test/website13.test.ts`): mixed digits,
  honorific words, the ruling heading, prefaces left out, related narrators,
  number search in three digit forms, the PDF list's order and names.
- The built app in a browser, phone and desktop, on data built from the live
  website (incremental, from the published build: nothing to refetch, the
  snapshot reused):
  - a narration opened with only «روایت کا حکم» open, «حوالہ جات» closed;
    tapping «[1]» opened it at note [1]; 18 honorifics carried their words;
  - home: 910 / 13 / 16 / 317, three samples on a phone, six narrators;
  - bottom bar صفحۂ اول، تلاش، احادیث، روات، مزید, «احادیث» lit on /chapters;
  - کتب خانہ: 16 books by name with covers, «دیکھیں» and «روایات»;
  - روات حدیث: 23 letters, groups jump; a narrator page with 12 related
    narrators and «اوپر جائیں»;
  - no console errors, no sideways scrolling.
- On the 12 Nov data, 648 of 910 narrations have a ruling heading (the website
  counted 649 that day). No heading ends in «حکم» without matching, so the
  difference is in the data each side counted, not the rule.
